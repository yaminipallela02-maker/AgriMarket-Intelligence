ABSTRACT


Agriculture remains the cornerstone of the Indian economy, engaging over 58% of the rural population. However, farmers continually face acute vulnerabilities due to extreme APMC Mandi price volatility, market arrival gluts, intermediary rent-seeking, and unscientific crop selection. This project, titled “AgriMarket Intelligence – Smart Agriculture & APMC Mandi Market Price Forecasting System,” presents an end-to-end data-driven decision support platform engineered using Machine Learning, Relational Databases (MySQL 8.0 / SQLite dual architecture), and Streamlit Cloud.

The platform operationalizes three core Machine Learning pipelines trained on real-world government datasets:

  • APMC Mandi Price Forecasting: An XGBoost Regressor trained on 6,687 official Agmarknet transaction records across Indian market yards to forecast future modal prices per Quintal and per Kilogram across 12 monthly horizons (R² > 0.94).
  • Arrival Demand Forecasting: An XGBoost Regressor (R² = 0.9965) forecasting seasonal arrival volume surges to help farmers avoid harvest supply gluts.
  • Scientific Crop Recommendation: A Random Forest Classifier achieving 94.81% accuracy across 2,700 agricultural scientific records spanning 22+ crops evaluated on soil Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), Soil pH, Temperature, Humidity, and Rainfall.

The system incorporates Firebase Mobile Phone OTP authentication with live countdown expiration and smart fallback, an interactive bilingual Voice & Text Assistant in Telugu and English featuring Web Speech recognition and audio Text-to-Speech (TTS), and a secure multi-table database architecture ensuring offline resilience and cloud scalability.


Keywords: APMC Mandi, XGBoost, Random Forest, Price Forecasting, Soil N-P-K, Bilingual Voice NLP, Streamlit, MySQL 8.0, Smart Agriculture.

CHAPTER 1: SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & METHODOLOGY


Architecture Overview

Tier 1 (Presentation Layer): Streamlit Responsive Web Application, HTML5 Web Speech Voice Recognition & Synthesis Engine, Firebase Phone OTP Component with 5-minute ticking countdown timer.

Tier 2 (Intelligence & ML Layer): Scikit-Learn Preprocessing Pipelines, XGBoost Price Regressor, XGBoost Demand Volume Forecaster, Random Forest Agronomic Classifier (94.81% Accuracy).

Tier 3 (Data Persistence Layer): Dual-layer Relational Database: Enterprise MySQL 8.0 with automatic SQLite fallback for high availability.
